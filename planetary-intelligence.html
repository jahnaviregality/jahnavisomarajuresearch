01 / Research signal

The question beneath the system

How can heterogeneous environmental streams be fused into interpretable anomaly judgments that scale across domains and tolerate partial failure?

Abstract, condensed from the supplied source

The Planetary Intelligence System (PIS) coordinates perception, geo-spatial, temporal-pattern, and hazard-domain agents across satellite imagery, ground sensors, weather telemetry, seismic instrumentation, and text. It uses a typed message bus, tiered memory, retrieval-augmented knowledge, capability routing, weighted consensus, and conflict resolution.

02 / Context

Why this work exists

05 / Evidence

What the paper actually reports

The paper reports F1 improvement of 9.4–16.7 percentage points, 31% lower end-to-end latency, and more than half reduction in false-positive rate; removing consensus or vector memory degraded accuracy most in the ablation.

Available evidence

The source reports evaluation across four simulated and two real-world-derived datasets, with F1 gains, latency reduction, and false-positive reduction versus named baselines. This page preserves those reported claims as source-stated results.
